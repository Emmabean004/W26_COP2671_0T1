Title: Pizza Panic

Overview:
Pizza Panic is a side view game where players control a chef who must collect Pizza falling from above, deliver them to hungry customers, and avoid bombs which are also falling from above. The goal is to get the highest score possible within the time limit or before the player hits too many bombs.



How to Play:

* Collect Pizzas by standing under them as they fall.
* Avoid standing under bombs as they fall.
* Throw the pizzas you've collected to the customers as they appear (It's okay if you miss customers and/or pizzas)



Controls:

* Left \& Right Arrow Keys Or WASD: Move Chef
* ESC: Open/close pause menu
* SPACE BAR: Throw Pizzas
* Title screen with UI
* Real-time countdown timer and score display
* Background music with separate sound effects
* Particle effects for bombs
